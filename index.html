BTS

  • It is a seven member South Korean boy band formed by Big Hit Entertainment in 2013.

  • They are widely recognized as one of the most successful and influential music acts in modern global pop history.

Music Styles and Themes

  1. Self-Expression and co-writing:Unlike many traditional k-pop groups,BTS members are heavily involved in song writing,composing,and producing their own music.
  2. Concept series:They structure their discography around overarching storytelling themes:
    • School Trilogy(2015-2016):Youth struggles and pressure in the education system.
    • The most beautiful moment in life/HYYH(2015-2016):Coming-of-age,anxiety,and the beauty/hardship of youth.
    • Wings(2016):Temptation and personal growth.
    • Love yourself trilogy(2017-2018):Self-love,mental health,and accceptance.
    • Map of the soul(2019-2020):Jungian psychology,identity,and personal reflection.
